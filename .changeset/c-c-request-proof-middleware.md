KYA-OS story C-C: every request proof is verified in the SPEC-ENTITY-CARD §11.2 order (`verifyRequestProof`), the legacy `org.kya-os/proof@1` key and `prf` accepted, the canonical key winning. A bearer token sent with a proof is its sender constraint: its `cnf.jkt` (`AuthResult.cnfJkt`, read from JWT and introspection claims) fuses with the proof's for L3; without it the proof is L3-minus. A chain without a proof is verified in full at most at L2. New `minimumLevel` (default `L3`), `tools[name].minimumLevel` and `l3MinusSatisfiesL3` (default `true`): a call below a tool's minimum, checked after the scopes, is a `LevelError` (`PROOF_LEVEL_INSUFFICIENT`, 401 `insufficient_user_authentication`) with `derivedLevel` and `requiredLevel`. Audit events and verification reports record the level, `fresh`, `proofAssurance`, L3-minus and `cnf` diagnostics; in A-1's typed events they are the `proof.verified` verification code (`KYAOS_PROOF_L3_MINUS`, `_CACHED`, `_CNF_UNFUSED`), and a level refusal is `authorization.denied` with reason `proof_level_insufficient`.
